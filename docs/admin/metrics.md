# Platform KPI Definitions & Telemetry Formulas

> **Mandate:** In compliance with prompt section #52, every metric displayed in the platform control plane is strictly defined and verifiable.

## Commercial & Financial Metrics

### Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- **Definition:** The total predictable monthly subscription revenue generated by active paid tenant organizations.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{MRR} = \sum_{s \in \text{Active Subscriptions}} \begin{cases} \text{price\_cents}(s) / 100 & \text{if billing\_interval} = \text{'monthly'} \\ (\text{price\_cents}(s) / 12) / 100 & \text{if billing\_interval} = \text{'annual'} \end{cases}$$
- **Exclusions:** Free/Developer Sandbox tiers ($0) and canceled/past-due subscriptions.

### Annual Run Rate (ARR)
- **Definition:** The annualized run-rate of current monthly recurring revenue.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{ARR} = \text{MRR} \times 12$$

## Operational & SRE Compliance Metrics

### Shift Handover Compliance SLA
- **Definition:** The percentage of shift handover reports that completed the mandatory dual-signature acceptance protocol.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Compliance Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Count of Handover records where } \text{is\_accepted} = \text{true}}{\text{Total Count of Handover records}} \right) \times 100$$
- **Target:** $\ge 99.5\%$

### Incident Resolution MTTR Rate
- **Definition:** Percentage of flagged SRE incident activities that have been formally resolved with a valid `resolved_at` timestamp.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Resolution Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Count of Activities where } \text{is\_incident} = \text{true} \text{ and } \text{resolved\_at IS NOT NULL}}{\text{Total Activities where } \text{is\_incident} = \text{true}} \right) \times 100$$
- **Target:** $\ge 95.0\%$

## Multi-Tenant Density Metrics

### Workspace Density Ratio
- **Definition:** Average number of operational workspaces provisioned per tenant organization.
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{Density} = \frac{\text{Total Workspaces}}{\text{Total Organizations}}$$
